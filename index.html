The Philosophy

True dermatological care extends beyond clinical treatment; it is an art of restoration and confidence. Dr. Sayantika Dutta brings world-class expertise to every consultation, seamlessly blending evidence-based British dermatological standards with a deeply personalized approach.

Her practice is designed as a serene sanctuary, where advanced medical technology meets unhurried, compassionate attention. Every treatment plan is meticulously curated to honor the unique biology and aesthetic goals of the individual.

Areas of Expertise

Bespoke solutions for medical and aesthetic dermatological needs.

Clinical Dermatology

Comprehensive diagnosis and management of complex skin conditions, including eczema, psoriasis, and autoimmune dermatoses, utilizing the latest global protocols.

Advanced Laser Therapy

Precision laser treatments for hair removal, vascular lesions, and pigmentation, administered with an emphasis on safety and optimal skin rejuvenation.

Acne & Scar Revision

Targeted, multi-modal therapies to resolve active acne and restore skin texture, minimizing scarring through evidence-based medical and procedural interventions.

Aesthetic Rejuvenation

Subtle, natural-looking anti-aging treatments designed to restore volume, refine contours, and enhance the skin's inherent radiance without altering facial identity.
